# APP-004 pre-activation clinical audit — 2026-08-31

**Scope:** current learner route in `onqol-vnext`, fixed preset `APP-004`, both Reference and Real-world hospital modes. `onqol-trainer` was used only to trace migrated provenance and withdrawn rules; its runtime was not merged into this audit.

**Verdict:** **BLOCK FROM TRAINING / NO-GO for a resident pilot.** The audience gate correctly prevents `learner_pilot` and production sessions, no clinical object is `approved_for_training`, numerical scoring is absent, and unapproved trajectories/treatment effects do not alter patient truth. However, the ordinary UI still launches the case as `internal_development` without an up-front limitation label, hard preoperative gates bypass the clinical governance status of their content, and the debrief turns pending preoperative/workflow items into learner “misses.” Clinical discharge readiness, natural history, postoperative recovery, analgesic response, prophylaxis regimen/timing, contraindications and dose branches are not calibrated.

## Evidence boundary

The external sources below support candidate principles, not automatic activation of exact simulator wording, thresholds, doses or KZ workflow:

1. WSES 2025: uncomplicated appendectomy may be delayed within 24 h; laparoscopy remains the standard approach; implementation must adapt to local resources. Podda et al., 2026, [JAMA Surgery](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamasurgery/article-abstract/2844195).
2. WSES 2020: laparoscopy preferred when expertise/equipment exist; pre-incision broad-spectrum prophylaxis; no postoperative antibiotics for uncomplicated appendicitis; routine drains discouraged. Di Saverio et al., 2020, [World Journal of Emergency Surgery / PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7386163/).
3. IDSA 2024: CT may be initial imaging in nonpregnant adults, but this is conditional, very-low-certainty guidance and US/MRI may precede CT depending on circumstances. [IDSA guideline](https://www.idsociety.org/practice-guideline/intra-abdominal-infections/).
4. Kazakhstan Order No. 78: the anesthesia standard is current but was amended in 2025; it supports anesthesiologist review after an emergency operative decision, while exact simulator blocking/documentary items still require current-text verification. Ministry of Health of Kazakhstan, 2023, updated 2025, [Adilet](https://www.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2300032409/info).

Medication-specific candidate evidence was additionally checked against ASHP/SIS/SHEA/IDSA surgical prophylaxis guidance (2013): appendectomy prophylaxis requires aerobic and anaerobic coverage; cefazolin monotherapy is not the cited appendectomy regimen. This is recorded as provenance evidence, not approval: [ASHP guideline](https://www.ashp.org/-/media/assets/policy-guidelines/docs/therapeutic-guidelines/therapeutic-guidelines-antimicrobial-prophylaxis-surgery.pdf).

Status meanings follow the requested A–G scheme. “Learner-visible” includes chat, refusal, mentor, debrief and generated patient truth. “Scoring” includes formative labels such as “Важные пропуски” and learning objectives, not only numeric points.

## Direct answers to the 20 audit questions

1. **Simulation constraints used as clinical thresholds:** yes. Pending preop content becomes a hard surgery gate and a debrief “miss”; KZ workflow items become formative omissions. The frozen natural course can also make delay look clinically harmless rather than merely unmodeled.

2. **Numbers without provenance:** yes. The major groups are APP-004 onset/pain distributions; HR/T/SBP/RR/WBC/neutrophils/CRP envelopes; fasting 4–10 h and 1–4 h; uniform symptom/imaging probabilities; lab/imaging/staff/transfer ETAs; 50% dynamic-resource probability; real-profile day/night hours; and generic clinical-engine defaults. The 24 h operative-delay candidate has guideline provenance but still lacks approved exact wording and KZ reconciliation. Medication doses other than the cefazolin fragment are intentionally null.

3. **Deterministic physiological responses needing bounded variation:** no active fixed response survives; that is good. The withdrawn hourly drift, fixed NSAID pain −3 and crystalloid HR/SBP deltas are gone. The remaining problem is stronger: biology/recovery is frozen, so reviewed bounded state transitions are still required.

4. **Invented probabilities:** no active perforation, drug-response, deterioration or complication probability is currently executed. Retired SSI 0.04/monitoring 0.09 remain audit-only. Hidden synthetic probabilities remain in uniform patient-fact picks and the 0.5 hospital-constraint generator; they must not be presented as epidemiology.

5. **Penalty for an evidence-based alternative:** there is no numeric penalty and open surgery is allowed. Formative pressure still exists: pending preop/KZ items and missing monitoring are labeled misses; unsupported medications force another choice because the simulator lacks content. These are platform/workflow judgments, not validated clinical incorrectness.

6. **Biological consequence for a workflow/regulatory omission:** no active case remains. The previous rule that inadequate monitoring increased SSI incidence was removed. KZ omissions are caught up by staff and have no biological effect. The defect is formative labeling, not biology.

7. **KZ-specific requirement presented as universal:** the content correctly marks ABO/Rh and coagulation as KZ hospital rules, but placing them under “Важные пропуски” still makes them look like resident correctness criteria. The Reference hospital is also hardcoded to KZ jurisdiction. Current Adilet text was amended in 2025, so exact applicability needs renewed verification.

8. **Learner-facing wording turning pilot boundaries into medical fact:** yes. “Эталонные условия,” “реалистичное” timing and a normal clinical chat run before any development/unreviewed warning. Frozen biology and zero active complications are not labelled during play.

9. **Rule blocking discharge, surgery or treatment without enough basis:** yes. Surgery is hard-blocked by consent, IV access, prophylaxis and anesthesiologist arrival even though their exact package is pending. Worse, any recognized prophylaxis order can unlock the OR despite invalid/unreviewed regimen and timing. Partial cecectomy and closing without appendectomy are blocked as not modeled. Clinical discharge is under-blocked rather than over-blocked because its criteria are skipped.

10. **Hidden defaults that are clinical decisions:** yes. Future state transitions default to probability 1 and time 0; treatment onset to 0; complication onset to 48 h; infusion without duration to bolus; monitoring to `ward_standard`. These are safe only while no clinical content is authoritative; activation must require explicit values.

11. **Do not admit to `approved_for_training` now:** all patient-generation truth; all physiology/lab ranges; natural and postoperative trajectories; treatment effects; prophylaxis agent/dose/route/timing/redosing/allergy/adjustment; analgesia/contraindications/rescue; fasting/aspiration/anesthesia; VTE; clinical discharge criteria; KZ workflow rules; operative challenge wording; and debrief correctness rules.

12. **Safe for pilot now:** only architecture-owned facts and transparently labelled simulation mechanics: event log, deterministic replay, requirement to clarify missing order parameters, separation of resource limitation from unsupported content, structural “operation is not complete / patient is still in OR or recovery,” absence of numeric scoring, and fail-closed learner/production audience gate. The actual app route remains internal-development only.

13. **May remain in engine but must not score:** 24 h timing candidate, CT/US alternatives, laparoscopic preference, drain, no routine postop antibiotics, early intake/mobilization, KZ workflow observations, hospital/resource delays and all proposed discharge criteria. They may be recorded/shadowed but not treated as correctness until reviewed.

14. **Surgeon review:** patient phenotype and operative truth; imaging pathway; timing from diagnosis/admission/commitment; operative vs nonoperative scope; laparoscopy/open; drain/lavage/partial cecectomy/closure without appendectomy; prophylaxis principle; postoperative antibiotics; source-control mapping; natural/postoperative course; monitoring; discharge criteria and follow-up wording.

15. **Anesthesia review:** hard preop set; IV access; fasting windows; aspiration-risk response; emergency fasting wording; anesthesia eligibility/airway scope; anesthesiologist-arrival gate; recovery exit; postoperative nausea/pain; urinary function relevance; VTE representation.

16. **Pharmacy/pharmacology review:** prophylaxis agents, complete regimens, dose, route, timing, redosing, beta-lactam allergy alternatives, renal/hepatic adjustment; every analgesic dose/route/onset/duration/contraindication/cumulative limit; opioid-like rescue and interactions; fluid-state response.

17. **Current Kazakhstan regulatory verification:** Order No. 78 exact amended text and scope; ABO/Rh and coagulation applicability; blood/transfusion rule linkage; current KZ appendicitis protocol versus WSES timing; KZ formulary/availability; site-specific contrast policy; all KZ-R1/R2/R3 resource and transfer assumptions.

18. **Code stricter than textual clinical specification:** hard preop blocking runs independently of pending governance status; debrief marks all preop items as misses; monitoring absence is a miss although standard ward monitoring is automatic; partial cecectomy/close-without-appendectomy are non-executable; treatment orders require drug+dose+route even where no approved dose exists. The clinical specification calls several of these pending, contextual or workflow-only.

19. **Mentor/debrief more categorical than clinical rule:** yes. The debrief calls pending preop items “important misses,” calls source control a success, and prescribes monitoring/preop learning objectives without an approved rule. Operative-option challenge text makes clinical assertions while the option is merely governance-blocked.
